## 2. Quick 3-Step Web Installation

### Step 1: Upload Files
1. Extract the downloaded zip archive.
2. Upload the files into your web root directory (e.g. `public_html` in cPanel, or `htdocs/realestate` in XAMPP).
3. Ensure your domain points to the project root (where `index.php` is located) or `public/`.

### Step 2: Open Installer
1. Open your browser and visit your site URL: `http://yourdomain.com/`
2. It will automatically redirect to the installer wizard: `http://yourdomain.com/install`

### Step 3: Enter Customer Details & Purchase Code
1. Enter your **Full Name**, **Valid Phone Number** (select country code from the dropdown), and **Email**.
2. Optional: Enter **Profession**, **Country**, and **City**.
3. Enter your official **Purchase Code** (e.g. `WEBOT-REAL-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).
4. Click **"Verify Code & Continue"**.

### Step 4: Enter MySQL Database Credentials
1. **Host**: `127.0.0.1` (or `localhost`)
2. **Port**: `3306`
3. **Database Name**: e.g. `realestate` (the installer will automatically create it if it doesn't exist!)
4. **Username**: `root` or your database username
5. **Password**: your database password
6. Click **"Test Connection"**, then click **"Start Installation & Migrate"**.
7. The installer will auto-migrate tables, generate application keys, seed data, and finalize your setup!
